## Replies and threads

Use **Reply in thread** on a message to keep your reply with that conversation.
To start a thread with a new message, select **Post as thread** in the composer
before you send it. The room stays open; select the message's thread link to open
the thread.

An open thread has its own address. Copy the browser's address to share it with
someone who has access to the room. Closing a thread returns you to the room;
it does not unfollow the conversation.

<p>If you prefer to keep the room visible while you reply, open
<a href={resolve('/chat/preferences/appearance')}>App Preferences → Appearance</a>
and set <strong>Thread pane</strong> to <strong>Side by side</strong>. When there is
not enough space, Chatto shows the thread over the room instead.</p>

## Keep track of conversations

Select **Follow thread** to keep a conversation in your **Threads** list. You can
follow a thread without posting in it.

In **Threads**, **Followed** shows the threads you follow. **Mine** shows the
ones you started and still follow. Unfollowing a thread removes it from both
views. Select **Unread** to find conversations with replies you have not read.

The search field checks messages and replies in your followed threads, including
direct-message threads. Search for words, a quoted phrase, or an author with
`from:username`. Search is available when your server supports and enables it.

## Move between rooms and unread activity

Press **Ctrl+K**, or **Command+K** on a Mac, to open the quick switcher. Search
there to move between rooms and servers without working through the sidebar.

Press **Alt+Shift+Down**, or **Option+Shift+Down** on a Mac, to go to the next
unread source across your signed-in servers. Chatto takes you to important
notifications first, then other notifications, then rooms with unread dots.
Your current message draft stays in place.

The bell in the app header opens notifications from all your signed-in servers.
Reading a room or thread marks notifications read only up to the messages you
can see. Opening an older message does not mark newer replies read.

For the available keyboard shortcuts, press **Ctrl+Shift+K**, or
**Command+Shift+K** on a Mac.

## Choose what notifies you

Open your server's **Notifications** settings to change alerts for mentions,
replies, reactions, and other activity. Each setting can apply to the whole
server, a room group, or one room.

- **Inherit** uses the setting from the room group or server.
- **Off** creates no notification or unread dot for that kind of activity.
- **Unread dot** marks the room without creating a notification.
- **In-app notification** adds an entry to the notification centre.
- **Push notification** also allows an alert on a device where you have enabled
  notifications.

For example, use **Unread dot** for new room messages and **Push notification**
for direct mentions if you want to check general chat in your own time.

<p>Push alerts also need device permission. On a phone, follow
<a class="link" href={withHelpServer(resolve('/help/[topic]', { topic: 'phone' }), page.url.searchParams.get('server'))}>Install Chatto on your phone</a>
to install the app and enable notifications. Your phone's notification settings
control whether names and message previews appear on the lock screen.</p>

## When the connection drops

Messages already loaded remain visible, but Chatto needs a server connection to
load more messages or send a reply. When the connection returns, it loads missed
updates.

If sending fails, your draft remains in the composer. It is not queued for
automatic delivery: check the connection and send it again. Installing Chatto on
your phone does not make your chat history available offline.
