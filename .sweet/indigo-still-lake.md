Refined the call controls in the app header and the call panel:

- The header names the call's room, with a status dot that glows while you speak and warns about a poor or lost connection. With more than one server, the server's icon comes first.
- Microphone, camera, and screen share show a green icon while they send media and a slashed icon while off, instead of bright green buttons.
- Leave call shows a red icon instead of a red button.
- The header buttons are flat and larger on touch devices.
- Screen share is hidden in browsers that cannot share a screen.
