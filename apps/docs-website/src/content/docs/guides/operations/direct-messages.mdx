---
title: Direct Messages
description: Start, archive, restore, and manage private one-to-one conversations.
---

Direct messages are private one-to-one conversations between members of the
same Chatto server. You can also start a conversation with yourself for notes.

## Start a conversation

Open a member's context menu from the member list, an @mention, or a message
author and select **Message**. Chatto reopens your existing conversation with
that member when one already exists. A newly started conversation appears in
the sidebar after its first message is sent.

Direct messages use the normal message, reply-attribution, reaction,
attachment, notification, and unread features. They do not support threads or
group conversations.

## Archive a conversation

Hover over a DM in the sidebar and select its archive button. The button uses
the row's trailing status position while you hover, replacing any unread or
notification indicator temporarily. You can also open the row's context menu
and select **Archive conversation**. The conversation disappears only from
your sidebar: the other participant is unaffected, and you keep your
membership, history, unread state, and notification settings.

An archived conversation remains available in the quick switcher and through
an existing direct link. When you open it, use the unarchive button in the
conversation header to restore it explicitly. Opening it alone does not
unarchive it.

Any new root message from either participant automatically restores the
conversation to your sidebar. Editing, reacting to, or retracting an existing
message does not restore it. If the message that restored the conversation is
later retracted, the conversation stays visible until you archive it again.

Archive controls require a server version that supports DM archive state. A
newer client connected to an older server continues to show DMs normally and
does not offer the controls.
