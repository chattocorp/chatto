This page shows how to set up typical communities. Each setup uses the
permission model in [Permissions & Roles](/guides/operations/permissions/):

- Roles, also `everyone`, only allow. An allow at the Server, Room group, or
  Room scope of a room gives the permission in that room.
- A Server-scope allow reaches every room. A room or room group cannot take it
  away.
- A deny on a single user is for rare exceptions.

To restrict a group of people, do not allow the access for `everyone`. Allow
it for a role that only these people have. The settings page of each room and
room group shows who can find and join it.

<Aside type="tip">
  Add the new allows before you remove the old ones. You can change a
  permission setting only if you have that permission at that scope, unless
  you are an owner. If you remove your own access first, you cannot add the
  allows after it.
</Aside>

## Open a Room or Room Group

New rooms and room groups start closed. Only owners in privileged mode and
the members that you add can reach them.

<Steps>

1. Open the room's settings → **Permissions**, or the room group's settings.
2. For `everyone`, allow `room.list` and `room.join`.
3. For `everyone`, allow what members can do in the room: `message.read`,
   `message.post`, `message.attach`, `message.react`, `message.echo`, and the
   `call.*` permissions.

</Steps>

- Open a room group to open all its rooms, also rooms that you add later. A
  room can add allows, but it cannot remove the allows of its room group.
- Do not allow `message.post` for `everyone` in a room group that contains a
  [read-only room](#read-only-room). On a new server, `#announcements` is in
  the `Lobby` group. Open the other rooms of `Lobby` one at a time, or move
  `#announcements` to a room group of its own.
- To open a room only to some people, allow these permissions for a role
  instead of `everyone`. See [Private room for one role](#private-room-for-one-role).
- Servers upgraded from earlier versions usually allow these permissions for
  `everyone` at Server scope. There, all rooms are open.

## Open Community

A new server opens only `#general` and `#announcements` to all members.

1. Open each room group to `everyone`. See
   [Open a room or room group](#open-a-room-or-room-group). Keep
   `#announcements` read-only. Do not use Server scope: a Server-scope allow
   also opens the rooms that you want to keep private later.
2. Assign `moderator` to the people who moderate. See
   [Set up a moderation team](#set-up-a-moderation-team).
3. Assign `admin` only to the people who manage the server.
4. To control who can make an account, use
   [invite-only account creation](/guides/operations/identity-login/#invite-only-account-creation).

## Set Up a Moderation Team

By default, `moderator` has `message.manage` and `room.remove-member` at
Server scope. Moderators can edit and delete the messages of other members,
remove members from rooms, and open the **Moderation** page.

- To let moderators see the member list and open member pages, allow
  `admin.view-users`.
- To let moderators restrict a member on the whole server, also allow
  `user.manage-permissions`. See
  [Handle a disruptive member](#handle-a-disruptive-member).
- Keep `moderator` above the roles of the members that moderators act on.
  Moderators can act only on accounts that
  [rank](/guides/operations/permissions/#role-order) below them.

Moderators must enable
[privileged mode](/guides/operations/permissions/#privileged-mode) to use
these permissions.

## Read-Only Room

Use this setup for announcements, rules, or news.

<Steps>

1. Open the room's settings → **Permissions**.
2. For `everyone`, allow `room.list`, `room.join`, `message.read`, and
   `message.react`. To let members reply in threads, also allow
   `message.post-in-thread`.
3. Allow `message.post` for each role that can post, such as `admin`.

</Steps>

- `everyone` must not have `message.post` at a scope that reaches the room.
  Do not allow it in the room's room group or at Server scope. If the room is
  in an open room group, move it to a room group that does not allow posting
  for `everyone`.
- Servers upgraded from earlier versions usually allow `message.post` and
  `message.post-in-thread` for `everyone` at Server scope. Remove these
  allows, and allow them in the room groups or rooms where members can post.
- The default `#announcements` room uses this setup.

## Members-Only Server

New accounts see only a few rooms, such as `#welcome` and `#rules`. After a
check, a moderator gives the account the `member` role. With the role, the
account can see and join the rooms that you open to `member`.

<Steps>

1. On the **Roles** page, create the role `member`. A new role starts lowest,
   below `moderator`.
2. Open the room groups for members to `member`: allow the permissions in
   [Open a room or room group](#open-a-room-or-room-group) for `member`, not
   for `everyone`.
3. Assign `member` to all current members who must keep access, also to
   owners and moderators. Owners have access without the role only in
   privileged mode.
4. Open `#welcome` and `#rules` to `everyone`. Make them universal, so that
   new accounts are in them immediately. `#announcements` is open to
   `everyone` on a new server.
5. Remove the allows of `room.list` and `room.join` for `everyone` from the
   room groups for members and from their rooms, such as `#general`. On
   servers upgraded from earlier versions, also remove them at Server scope.
6. To let moderators assign `member`, allow `role.assign` and
   `admin.view-users` for `moderator`. Moderators assign the role on the
   member's **Roles** page in privileged mode.

</Steps>

Universal rooms follow `room.join`. When you assign `member`, Chatto adds the
account to the universal rooms. When you remove the role, Chatto removes the
account from them. Removing the allow does not remove explicit room members. Examine the
member lists of all rooms, because universal rooms can also have explicit
members.

Invite links do not assign roles. You assign roles one account at a time, or
with `chatto operator user role add` (see
[Operator CLI](/guides/operations/operator-cli/)). Make this setup before you
invite many members.

### Guests Who Can Read but Not Post

Do steps 1, 3, and 6. Then, in the room groups:

1. Allow `room.list`, `room.join`, and `message.read` for `everyone`.
2. Allow `message.post`, `message.attach`, `message.react`, and
   `message.echo` for `member`.
3. Remove these posting permissions for `everyone` in the rooms that allow
   them, such as `#general`. On servers upgraded from earlier versions, also
   remove them and `message.post-in-thread` for `everyone` at Server scope.

Guests can still use direct messages, because a new server allows
`message.post` for `everyone` at Direct messages scope. To stop this, see
[Limit direct messages](#limit-direct-messages). To let only members use
calls, allow the `call.*` permissions in the room groups only for `member`.

## Private Room for One Role

Use this setup for staff rooms, team rooms, or rooms for supporters.

<Steps>

1. Create a role, such as `engineering`, and assign it.
2. Make sure that no allow of `room.list` or `room.join` for `everyone`
   reaches the room. Put the room in a room group that is not open to
   `everyone`, and do not allow these permissions for `everyone` at Server
   scope.
3. In the room's permissions, allow `room.list`, `room.join`, and the content
   permissions, such as `message.read` and `message.post`, for
   `engineering`.
4. Remove the current room members who must not have access.
5. Turn on **Universal** to add each account with the role to the room.

</Steps>

The room's settings must show that only `engineering` can join it. Other roles
need their own allow. You cannot close a room with a setting in the room: an
allow for `everyone` in the room group or at Server scope opens it. Owners can
access the room only in privileged mode, unless they have the role or their
own allow in the room.

## Private Room Group for a Team

To make all rooms of a room group private, use the steps for one room at
Room group scope. Open the room group's settings, and allow `room.list`,
`room.join`, and the content permissions for the role. Do not allow them for
`everyone` there or at Server scope. Each room in the group uses these
settings. A room can add allows, for example to open one room of the group to
`everyone`.

## Events and Courses

<Steps>

1. Create a role for each cohort or track, such as `cohort-2026`.
2. Create a room group for each cohort and make it private for the role. See
   [Private room group for a team](#private-room-group-for-a-team).
3. Add a universal, read-only `#announcements` room for all participants. See
   [Read-only room](#read-only-room).
4. Add sidebar links to schedules, recordings, and forms.
5. When the event ends, archive its rooms. See
   [Archive rooms](/guides/operations/community-structure/#archive-rooms).

</Steps>

## Question and Support Rooms

To keep each question and its answers together, set the room's
[Threading Mode](/guides/operations/community-structure/#threading-mode) to
**Required**. To slow down a busy room, use
[Slow Mode](/guides/operations/community-structure/#slow-mode).

## Limit Calls to Some People

`everyone` must not have these permissions at a scope that reaches the room.
A new server allows them for `everyone` only in `#general` and at Direct
messages scope. Servers upgraded from earlier versions allow them at Server
scope.

- **Calls only for a role:** in the room, allow `call.start`, `call.join`,
  `call.voice`, `call.camera`, and `call.screenshare` for the role.
- **Talks with an audience:** in the room, allow `call.join` for `everyone`.
  Allow `call.start`, `call.voice`, `call.camera`, and `call.screenshare` for
  a `speaker` role. Everyone who can join the call can listen.

Members without `call.join` can still see that a call is active. See
[Calls](/guides/infrastructure/voice-calls/#call-permissions).

## Limit Direct Messages

Set `message.post` for `everyone` at Direct messages scope to **Inherit**.
Then allow it at Direct messages scope for the roles that can use direct
messages. Accounts without the permission cannot start direct messages or post
in them.

A Server-scope allow also applies in direct messages. Servers upgraded from
earlier versions usually allow `message.post` for `everyone` at Server scope.
Remove it there, and allow it in the room groups or rooms where members post.

## Team Mentions

To let members mention a team, such as `@engineering`, open the role on the
**Roles** page and select **Allow people to ping this role**. A role mention
notifies the members of the role who are in the room.

## Delegate a Room Group

Use this setup to let a team lead manage the rooms of a team.

<Steps>

1. Create a role, such as `project-lead`. Move it above the roles that the
   lead must configure.
2. In the settings of the room group, allow `room.manage` and `room.create`
   for `project-lead`.
3. To let the lead moderate, also allow `message.manage` and
   `room.remove-member` in the room group.

</Steps>

To delegate one room, allow `room.manage` and the moderation permissions in
the room. Holders must enable privileged mode to use these permissions. A room
manager can change role settings only for permissions that they have at that
scope. Apart from `everyone`, they can change only roles below their highest
role.

## Add a Bot to One Room

<Steps>

1. In the bot's **Permissions**, allow the permissions that the bot needs at
   Room scope for that room.
2. Add the bot to the room with the **Joined** row in the bot's
   **Permissions**.

</Steps>

A bot never has more permissions than its owner. In a private room, the owner
must also have these permissions there, for example through the role of the
room. See [Bot accounts](/guides/integrations/bot-accounts/).

## Handle a Disruptive Member

- **Remove the member from one room:** select **Remove from room** for the
  member. Set a room suspension to stop them from joining again. The
  suspension can end at a set time. You need `room.remove-member`.
- **Stop the member from posting everywhere:** on the member's
  **Permissions** page, deny `message.post`, `message.post-in-thread`, and
  `message.post-in-interactions` at Server scope. You can also deny
  `message.attach`, `message.react`, `message.echo`, and `call.join`. First
  remove room or room-group allows on that member, because they replace the
  Server denies in those places. To end the restriction, remove the denies.
  You need `admin.view-users`, `user.manage-permissions`, and each of these
  permissions at Server scope.
- **Delete the account:** you need `user.delete-any`. On a server with open
  account creation, the person can make a new account. Use
  [invite-only account creation](/guides/operations/identity-login/#invite-only-account-creation)
  to prevent this.

Each of these actions needs a higher
[rank](/guides/operations/permissions/#role-order) than the member. Chatto
has no server-wide timeout. Use a timed room suspension, or remove the
denies when the restriction must end.

## Limit an Account to Its Own Conversations

Allow `message.read-interactions` and `message.post-in-interactions` instead
of `message.read` and `message.post`. The account can then read and reply only
in threads that it started, where another account mentioned it, or where it
received a direct message.

This works directly for bots, because bots do not get permissions from
`everyone`. For a person, also deny `message.read` and `message.post` on the
account. See [Bot accounts](/guides/integrations/bot-accounts/).
