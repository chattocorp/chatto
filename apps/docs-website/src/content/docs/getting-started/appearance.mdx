---
title: Appearance
description: Choose the app theme, contrast, accent colour, background colour, and surface depth for this browser.
---

Open **App Preferences → Appearance** from the gear in the Application Header.

In **Theme**, select **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. System follows your
browser or operating system setting.

In **Theme customisation**, under **Accent colour**, select Blue, Cyan, Teal, Green,
Amber, Orange, Pink, Violet, or Grey. Cyan is the default. The selected colour has a check mark.
You can also use the arrow keys when the colour picker has keyboard focus.

Below the accent colours, select a background tone. The tone tints
backgrounds, surfaces, and text. Light and dark mode each keep their own tone.
The picker shows the tones for the active theme; to choose the other tone,
change the theme first. Grey is the light default and Neutral is the dark
default.

Under **UI Style**, move **Depth** to set the strength of decorative bevels and
inset shading. **50%** is the default. **0%** removes them. **100%** makes
them stronger and wider. Changes animate unless your system requests reduced
motion.

In the same section, move **Contrast** to adjust text, surface, and background
contrast. **50%** keeps the original appearance. At **100%**, light mode uses
black text on a white background. Dark mode uses white text on a black
background. At **0%**, headings and other text become much softer. The contrast
changes while you move the slider. Both sliders move in 10% steps.

Changes apply immediately to every registered server in this app. The browser
saves your choices for future visits. They do not sync to another browser or
device. Accent colours change controls and highlights; status and warning
colours retain their meaning.
