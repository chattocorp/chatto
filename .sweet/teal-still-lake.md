Sign-in with providers whose pages isolate the sign-in window, such as Bluesky, no longer stops silently. Chatto now waits for the result, and the waiting notice has a Cancel button. All sign-in windows are also larger, so provider pages such as Bluesky's fit without scrolling.

Closing the sign-in window in Chatto Desktop lets you try again immediately, including when the provider isolates its page. Browser tabs also detect closure while Chatto prepares the sign-in URL. On the login and registration pages, select **Try Again** to reopen provider sign-in in one click, including in an installed web app.
