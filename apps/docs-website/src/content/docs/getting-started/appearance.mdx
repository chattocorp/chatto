---
title: Appearance
description: Choose the app theme, accent colour, and surface depth for this browser.
---

Open **App Preferences → Appearance** from the gear in the Application Header.

In **Theme**, select **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. System follows your
browser or operating system setting.

In **Accent colour**, select Blue, Cyan, Teal, Green, Amber, Orange, Pink,
Violet, or Grey. Cyan is the default. The selected colour has a check mark.
You can also use the arrow keys when the colour picker has keyboard focus.
The preview shows the selected accent on a button, link, and toggle.

In **Surface depth**, select **Flat**, **3D**, or **Very 3D**. **3D** is the
default. Flat removes decorative bevels and inset shading. Very 3D makes them
stronger and wider. Changes animate unless your system requests reduced motion.

Changes apply immediately to every registered server in this app. The browser
saves your choices for future visits. They do not sync to another browser or
device. Accent colours change controls and highlights; status and warning
colours retain their meaning.
