The 0.6 client can still connect to 0.5.0 servers. The Jobs page and role selection in Shield Mode require a 0.6 server. For servers still on 0.4, upgrade to at least 0.5.0 for discovery. Members whose 0.4 browser sessions never migrated through 0.5 must sign in again. See [API compatibility](/guides/integrations/api-compatibility/).
